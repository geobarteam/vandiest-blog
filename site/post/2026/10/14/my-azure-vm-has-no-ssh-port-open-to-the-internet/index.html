My Azure VM has no SSH port open to the internet. I still manage it from my sofa.

Geoffrey Vandiest14 OCT 2026

The trick is one of the tools I like very much: Tailscale.

My AI assistant Zyggy runs on that VM. With Tailscale, my laptop, my phone and the VM are on the same private network, wherever I am. No VPN to configure, no firewall rules to write.

You install it, you log in, and it just works. I almost forgot it was there.

The bots scanning port 22 all day can keep trying. Nobody opens the door.

And it's free for personal use.
